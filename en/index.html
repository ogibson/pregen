AI and genomics for accessible cancer prognosis

PREDICTIVE GENOMIC INTELLIGENCE
What we do

At PreGen, we use artificial intelligence to find biomarkers that predict tumor aggressiveness and response to treatment.

Why does prognosis matter?

When cancer is diagnosed, it is essential to understand the tumor’s biology to find the right treatment for each patient. That answer determines the choice between active surveillance and treatments with significant side effects.

Why PreGen?

The genomic tests that guide these decisions today cost thousands of dollars and are processed in centralized laboratories. PreGen develops gene signatures that can be measured with standard PCR, in local laboratories, so that precision prognosis is faster and accessible.

Shorter time to results

Processed in local laboratories, with no shipping abroad.

Lower costs

Uses PCR equipment already available in the region’s laboratories.

The sample stays in the country

Tissue is analyzed in the country where the biopsy was taken.

Better clinical decisions

Helps choose between active surveillance and treatment.

Our product

PreGen Prostate

PreGen Prostate is a prognostic test that measures a 10-gene signature by RT-qPCR on the diagnostic biopsy to estimate the tumor’s risk of progression.
