Precision in Cancer Prognosis

Each year, over one million men are diagnosed with prostate cancer. Current tools cannot reliably predict who will experience aggressive disease and who will not. PreGen leverages molecular signatures to provide clarity, guiding clinicians to deliver the right treatment at the right time.

Biopsy-driven Molecular Insights

By analyzing gene expression signatures directly from routine biopsies, PreGen’s platform predicts disease evolution with unprecedented precision. Starting with prostate cancer, our technology is scalable across multiple cancer types worldwide.
